Interviewer: Looking back, is there a moment you'd have handled differently? Participant: Maybe I could have pushed radiology harder given how backed up we were. But on the clinical reasoning itself, I wouldn't change much. The disposition at the end is probably where I sat longest and still feel least certain—it was a genuinely close call, not an obvious one.
