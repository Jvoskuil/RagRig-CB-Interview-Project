That's important because ventilation-limited burning distorts your pattern reading—you don't get a clean V-pattern pointing straight at an origin. Participant: Get a defensible origin and cause determination that would hold up for the insurer and, if it came to it, in a legal setting. Then came witness statements, which didn't line up cleanly with each other or with the dispatch log. Interviewer: What made the panel side win out over the fryer side, given both were physically plausible?
