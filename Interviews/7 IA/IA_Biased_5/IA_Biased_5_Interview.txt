Interviewer: What alternatives did you consider there?

Participant: We talked briefly about spreading collection across the whole event footprint instead of narrowing in, since the source had zero track record. Participant: Yeah, actually. Later that first day a partial SIGINT hit came back, but it was in a different sector, about two miles up the route from the corridor he named.
